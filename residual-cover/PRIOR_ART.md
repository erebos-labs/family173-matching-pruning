## Classical graph dependencies

The only classical named results used in the new proof are:

* The augmenting-path characterization of maximum matchings, attributed
  to Claude Berge, *Two theorems in graph theory*, PNAS 43(9) (1957),
  842-844, DOI 10.1073/pnas.43.9.842. The exact formulation used is:
  a finite matching is maximum iff no augmenting path exists. NOTE.md
  proves this by symmetric differences, so no inaccessible text is a
  hidden dependency.
* The finite bipartite matching-cover theorem of Denes Konig, cited in
  the pinned bibliography as *Graphok es matrixok*, Matematikai es
  Fizikai Lapok 38 (1931), 116-119. The exact formulation used is equality
  of maximum matching cardinality and minimum vertex-cover cardinality.
  NOTE.md supplies its alternating-reachability proof and checks the
  finite, bipartite hypotheses explicitly.

The ordered selector's monotonicity is proved from elementary exposure
monotonicity using those graph arguments. No theorem about matrix rank,
linear representations, determinants, generic coefficients, kernel
spaces, or representable matroids is invoked to prove the conversion.
The proof does not rely on Rado's theorem or a Dulmage-Mendelsohn theorem
whose reduction/hypotheses would otherwise need checking.
