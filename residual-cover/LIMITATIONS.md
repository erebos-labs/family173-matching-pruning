# Limits

The ordinary unbounded claim is the residual-cover theorem on the exact stated
four-corner geometry and its explicitly inherited corollaries. Neither a
finite certificate count nor an AI-assisted review replaces the proof.

Novelty and priority are unestablished. The inequalities were previously proved
by the rank argument. No comprehensive literature audit or full Seymour proof
review accompanies this addendum.

No Lean theorem, compilation or axiom audit is delivered. An accessible compiler
was not located in the selected preparation environment; no toolchain was
installed. This says nothing about availability on other hosts.

The independent verifier does not import a matching optimizer, but there is no
formal theorem connecting JSON/Python encodings to the mathematics. Gamma
normalization is checked in its final certified state; every intermediate
Gamma augmentation is not exported. B-extension paths are exported and replayed.

The constructed cover may exceed the minimum N0 cover. One shared total order
is required. Original Z,H stay fixed. Tagged roles remain distinct even when
coordinates coincide. Runtime for large instances was not benchmarked.

Local preparation checks and hosted CI results are distinct evidence. Actual
CI outcomes are available on the repository Actions page; no human referee or
formal-verification claim follows from those runs.
