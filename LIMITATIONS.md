# Scope and limitations

The theorem is exactly the arbitrary-relation statement in MATHEMATICAL_NOTE.md.
Loops, opposite pairs, coordinate coincidences and empty families are allowed;
R,C and the two Z copies are distinguished. Z,H are fixed from original conflicts.
q counts distinct uncovered original Z points. The optimum concerns d-q,
not minimum raw deletion count alone.

No mathematical gap was identified by the separate agent proof review and
independent archive/code audit. These are AI-assisted reviews, not human referee
review or formal verification.

Finite certificate replay and direct enumeration verify the implementation on
specified inputs. They are not the proof for arbitrary finite X. The programs
are not formally verified. The checker certifies graph optima with matching and
equal-size covers, independently of the constructor algorithm.

The explicit all-n sharp family proves coefficient 1 optimal even with a fixed
additive allowance for any smaller coefficient. Its acyclic relation is an
example and adds no restriction to the general theorem.

Direct pruning enumeration is exponential and bounded; the checker itself does
not require that enumeration for larger certificates. Intended Python 3.10+
portability is covered by bounded CI; actual run results are separately logged.

Novelty and priority are not established. Preparation consulted the primary
locations listed in provenance/PRIMARY_SOURCES.json, with the stated read scopes.
No comprehensive literature or downstream formalization search is claimed.
The historical full-volume Konig scan was not read; the note proves the needed
matching-cover argument itself. Related literature is not a mathematical premise.

No Lean theorem, compilation, imported-dependency or axiom audit is supplied.
The full Seymour manuscript, unrelated reductions and downstream graph claims
are outside this note and audit.

Only the pinned pruning source, paper README and licence are redistributed.
This is not a full upstream paper build; the figure input is absent.
Upstream source bytes and attribution are preserved.

The public package is a clean edition rather than the original preparation ZIP.
Its versioned hashes detect byte changes, not mathematical correctness or
malicious replacement of an entire package and its manifest.
