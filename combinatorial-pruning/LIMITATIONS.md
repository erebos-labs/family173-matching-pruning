# Limits of the partial addendum

The proven claims are exactly exposure transfer, mandatory core endpoints,
saturated-vertex extension and the core-deletion identity in NOTE.md. Loops,
opposite arrows, coordinate coincidences and empty cases are allowed. Tagged
roles are separate and corner sets come from original conflicts.

The full rank-free inequality and residual ell+t-|Z| accounting remain unresolved.
Finite routing tests do not close that step. The existing rank proof remains
the complete baseline.

A supplied Gamma matching may be used as a construction hint. The exported
trace starts at a certified maximum matching; the initial hint-to-maximum
augmentations are not exported. No stronger trace guarantee is claimed.
Extension preserves saturated vertices; edges can change.

Finite tests support the checker, not a theorem for every finite X. Direct
pruning enumeration is exponential and limited to twenty original vertices.
The 4000-case replay is a seeded sample, not an atlas, classification or
exhaustive census. Novelty and priority are unestablished.

The hardened decoder rejects non-integer certificate coordinate/schema aliases.
The implementation remains ordinary Python and is not formally verified.
Malformed structural inputs can exit with a Python exception; nonzero rejection
is the interface, not a security-service guarantee.

No Lean theorem, compile or axiom audit is delivered. No installed toolchain
was located during bounded local inspection and no new installation occurred.
No human referee review or endorsement of the full Seymour conjecture follows.

Only the pinned pruning source and licence are copied from upstream. Figure
dependencies are absent; this is not a full paper build. External matching
literature is cited with its access limits, not redistributed. See NOTICE.md.
